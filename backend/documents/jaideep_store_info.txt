JAIDEEP GENERAL STORE - CUSTOMER SUPPORT INFORMATION

Store Overview:
Jaideep General Store is a local retail and grocery shop providing daily household products, packaged foods, personal care items, beverages, dairy products, stationery, and general utility items to customers.

Store Timings:
The store operates from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day.

Home Delivery:
Home delivery is available within a 5 kilometer radius.
Delivery time is usually between 30 minutes to 2 hours depending on product availability and location.

Order Support:
Customers can place orders through phone call or WhatsApp support.

Payment Methods:
The store accepts:
- Cash
- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card

Refund Policy:
Products can be returned within 2 days only if:
- Product is damaged
- Wrong product delivered
- Expired item delivered

Refunds are not available for:
- Opened packaged food
- Personal hygiene products
- Dairy products

Replacement Policy:
Damaged or defective products can be replaced within 48 hours after purchase.

Cancellation Policy:
Orders can only be cancelled before dispatch.

Customer Support:
Customer support is available from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Popular Product Categories:
- Grocery Items
- Snacks and Beverages
- Rice and Flour
- Dairy Products
- Personal Care Products
- Cleaning Supplies
- School Stationery
- Kitchen Essentials

Emergency Support:
Customers can contact the store immediately for urgent delivery issues or missing items.

Special Services:
The store also provides monthly household package delivery for regular customers.

Discount Information:
Regular customers may receive seasonal discounts and festival offers.

Important Note:
Customers are advised to check expiry dates during product delivery.
